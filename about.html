About Volleyball

Learn the rules, positions, and skills of the game

Positions

Setter: Sets the ball for teammates to attack.

Outside Hitter: Attacks the ball and helps in defense.

Middle Blocker: Blocks opponent attacks near the net.

Opposite Hitter: Attacks the ball right-side and blocks.

Libero: Defensive specialist who receives and digs the ball.

Rules

Basic Rules – Understand the flow of the game.

Scoring – Points are earned by making the ball touch the opponent's court.

Serving – The rally starts with a good serve.

Rotation – Players rotate clockwise after winning the serve.

Fouls – Illegal actions result in loss of points.

Three-Touch Rule – A team can touch the ball a maximum of three times.

Skills

Serving: Starts the rally by delivering the ball.

Passing: Receives the serve or attack and keeps the rally.

Setting: Sets the ball accurately for an attack.

Spiking: Attacks the ball hard to score a point.

Blocking: Stops the opponent attack at the net.
